Voice handover — check

One line at a time, always. When a screen replaces the line before it, the old line must stop and stay stopped — it must not hand itself to the device engine and say itself again over the top of the new one, and it must not report that it finished when it was cut off.

Run this on the demo phone. It depends on real audio timing — how long play() takes to actually start — which is exactly what a desktop browser gets wrong. Turn the volume up: you should hear single lines with clean gaps, never two voices at once.
